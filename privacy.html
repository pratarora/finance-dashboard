Privacy Policy

CHF Financial & Savings Dashboard · Effective date: 26 November 2026

Short version: the dashboard runs entirely in your web browser. The developer does not collect, receive, store or have access to your financial data. If you choose to use Google Drive, your data is encrypted in your browser and saved only in your own Google Drive.

1. Who we are

The CHF Financial & Savings Dashboard (the "App") is a personal bookkeeping tool published by Prateek Arora (the "developer"). Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's issue tracker at github.com/pratarora/finance-dashboard/issues.

2. What the App does with your data

You load bank-transaction and credit-card CSV files into the App. The App reads these files, categorizes the transactions and draws charts, all locally in your browser using code that is downloaded when you open the page. Your files and the resulting data are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer. The developer runs no backend and has no database of user data.

The data the App handles on your device includes the transactions in your files (dates, merchants, descriptions, amounts, account and card references as they appear in your files), the categories you choose, and category rules you create.

3. Where your data can be stored

You decide how, or whether, your data is kept. Each option is optional:

  • Nowhere (default): the data exists only in the open browser tab and disappears when you close it.
  • In your browser: if you tick "Remember data in this browser", a copy is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Unticking the box erases this copy. You can also clear it with "Clear Data".
  • In a file you choose: "Link data file" and "Backup JSON" write your data to a file on your own computer. These files are not encrypted.
  • In your Google Drive: if you click "Connect Google Drive", the App stores an encrypted file (named like finance_data.enc.json) in your own Google Drive. See section 4.

4. Google Drive and Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the App uses Google Sign-In and requests a single permission (scope): https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission lets the App see and manage only the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot read your other files or folders.

  • What is stored: one encrypted file per dataset you create. The contents are encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from a passphrase you choose) before upload. Google and the developer see only encrypted data.
  • Your passphrase is never sent anywhere and is not stored by the App. It cannot be recovered if you forget it.
  • Access token: the sign-in token is kept in memory only for the current session and is revoked when you disconnect or close the page. The App does not store it.
  • No access by the developer: your Drive data is accessed only by code running in your own browser. The developer has no server-side access to your Google account or your Drive.
  • Use of Google user data: the App uses data from Google APIs solely to save and load your encrypted dataset for you. It is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with third parties, and is not read by humans.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Third-party resources

To work, the page loads open-source libraries (Chart.js and PapaParse) from the jsDelivr content delivery network, and Google's sign-in library from Google. When your browser fetches these, those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies. They do not receive your financial data from the App. If the App is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may also log standard web-server request data when the page is loaded.

6. Cookies, analytics and tracking

The App uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It sets no cookies of its own. Browser storage is used only for the options described in section 3 and for remembering your "Remember data" preference.

7. Retention and deletion

  • The developer retains no copy of your data because the developer never receives it.
  • To delete data in your browser, untick "Remember data in this browser" or use "Clear Data", or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To delete data in Google Drive, delete the finance_data*.enc.json file(s) from your Drive.
  • To remove the App's access to your Google account, visit your Google Account's third-party access settings and remove the App.

8. Security

Data stays on your device or in your own Drive, and Drive data is encrypted before upload. No system is perfectly secure: you are responsible for your device, your browser, your passphrase and any backup files you create. Do not use the App on computers you do not trust, and untick "Remember data in this browser" on shared computers.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children, and the developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
